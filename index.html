About Us

Trusted Civil & Building Construction Experts Since 2011

HELPO ECO GREEN LTD was established in 2011 as the partner private company of HELP-O NGO Sri Lanka, registered under the Companies Act No. 07 of 2007 (Section 07) with the Registrar of Companies, Sri Lanka. We deliver quality civil and building construction, combining solid engineering practices with sustainable, eco-conscious methods — while also helping civil engineers, quantity surveyors, planners and homeowners apply anaerobic digestion to manage waste, generate energy and improve sanitation.

  • Established in 2011 as the partner company of HELP-O NGO Sri Lanka
  • 100+ projects delivered across Sri Lanka and abroad
  • Qualified, experienced technical and engineering staff

Partners

Sri Lanka Nature Forum (SLNF)

Registered under Sri Lanka's Social Welfare Act in 2007, SLNF coordinates capacity-building work for member organisations and links to the UNDP/SGP/GEF Program. HELP-O is an active member.

CITYNET

The Regional Network of Local Authorities for the Management of Human Settlements, committed to improving the sustainability of human settlements across the Asia-Pacific. HELP-O NGO has been a member since 1996, with staff trained through CITYNET programs.

United Nations Development Programme (UNDP)

Operating in Sri Lanka since 1967, UNDP supports the country's Millennium Development Goals and poverty reduction agenda. HELP-O has worked with UNDP on community development projects.

National Solid Waste Management Support Center (NSWMSC)

Established in 2007 to help local authorities address solid waste management, following a JICA study. HELP-O has completed many biogas projects with NSWMSC's financial support.
